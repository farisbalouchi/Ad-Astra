Making Space Travel Possible

Who are we?

Ad Astra is a fictional international organisation dedicated to exploring and eventually settling space. We imagine teams based in countries like Germany 🇩🇪, the USA 🇺🇸, Canada 🇨🇦, China 🇨🇳, India 🇮🇳, and Australia 🇦🇺, working together on the idea of humans living beyond Earth. Space agencies like NASA and private companies like SpaceX are already working on the technology this would take — but the challenges are huge: radiation, extreme temperatures, limited resources, and enormous distances between planets. This site imagines what an organisation tackling those problems, with Mars as its main focus.

What is our mission?

Our (fictional) mission is to make space travel more accessible and sustainable over time, and to develop the technology needed for humans to live beyond Earth. We imagine an organisation built around international collaboration — combining research, engineering, and long-term planning to push toward a future where humanity isn't limited to one planet.
